How It Works

From
Raw to Final

Four stages, one dedicated editor. Transparent timelines, no black boxes, no surprise invoices.

Footage Review & Brief

You send raw footage and a detailed brief — we review everything, flag issues, and plan the edit structure before touching a timeline.

First Cut Assembly

Refinement & Polish

Revisions, color grading, motion graphics, sound design, and final polish. Every frame gets attention.

Final Delivery

Platform-ready exports in every resolution and format you need — plus source files and project archives.

Discipline 05 — Overview

A Video Editing Agency For Every Format

Acidic Studios is a video editing agency built for brands that publish everywhere at once. Our UGC video editing and short form video editing teams work alongside our long form video editors and YouTube automation editing specialists, so every deliverable — from a 15-second Reel to a 40-minute long form video editor cut — comes out of the same pipeline and the same brand voice.

As an ad video editing service, we build creatives engineered for Meta, TikTok, and YouTube ad performance, backed by a data-informed editing process rather than guesswork. Need channel management on top of the cuts? Our YouTube automation editing service bundles thumbnails, SEO metadata, and scheduling so your channel runs like a growth system, not a side project.
