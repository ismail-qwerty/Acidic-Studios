WHAT WE OFFER

The practical things that shape how it actually feels to work here. Reviewed every year, adjusted when they need to be.

Time

Four-Day Week

Mon–Thu, year-round. Fridays are protected — no meetings, no expectations, no email.

Ownership

Profit Share

After your first year, a share of studio profit is paid out based on tenure, not title.

Growth

Learning Budget

£2,000 per year for courses, books, conferences, or studio visits — yours to spend, no approval.

Rest

Sabbatical

A paid four-week sabbatical at the five-year mark, on top of your regular leave.

Equipment

Your Setup

Choose the machine, display, and tools that suit how you work. We cover it, fully.

Location

Remote, Done Right

Two in-person studio weeks per year — one in spring, one in autumn. Everything else is wherever you are.

OUR HIRING PROCESS

Four steps, usually three weeks end to end. We aim to be quick and honest at every stage.

01 — Application

Tell Us About Your Work

A short form: a few questions, a link to your portfolio or recent work. No cover letters required.

02 — Conversation

A 30-Minute Call

An informal conversation with the person you'd be working closest with. No trick questions.

03 — Practical

A Paid Working Session

Two hours on real brief material, paid at your rate. We see how you think; you see how we work.

04 — Decision

A Clear Answer

We come back to you within five working days, with a decision and honest feedback either way.
