The Process

Five Stages,
Zero Guesswork

One dedicated build team. Transparent timelines, no black boxes, no surprise invoices.

Step 01

Map The Workflow

We audit the process the agent will own — inputs, decisions, tools, and where humans need to stay in the loop.

Step 02

Build The Brain

The agent is trained on your data, docs, and tone, with guardrails set for what it can and can't do.

Step 03

Wire It In

Connected to your CRM, calendar, or helpdesk, then stress-tested against real conversations before launch.

Step 04

Go Live

Rolled out to your channel of choice — site, WhatsApp, phone line, or all three at once.

Step 05

Keep It Sharp

We track conversations, catch failure points, and retrain the agent as your product or offer changes.
