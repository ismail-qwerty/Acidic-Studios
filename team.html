§ 03 — How We Work

BRIEF TO DELIVERY

Four stages, two people, zero hand-offs in between.

Step 1

Brief

A direct conversation with a founder — not a form that gets forwarded three times.

Step 2

Plan

Scope, timeline, and price agreed upfront by the people who'll actually do the work.

Step 3

Build

Production, review, and revision — both founders stay hands-on until it's right.

Step 4

Ship

Delivered, signed off, and supported by the same two people who scoped it.

§ 04 — The Studio

An AI Creative Agency Run By Two People

Acidic Studios is a digital production studio and AI creative agency covering AI content production, video editing, web design, and brand identity design under one small, founder-led team. There's no roster of account managers between you and the work — just the two people who plan, produce, and sign off on everything we ship.

Our video editing agency work spans UGC video editing, short form video editing, and YouTube automation editing, while our web design agency side handles conversion-focused websites and headless website development. See the full breakdown on our video editing and websites pages.
