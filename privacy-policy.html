When you contact us, book a call, or apply for a role, we collect the information you submit directly — name, email, company, and project details. We also collect standard analytics data (pages visited, browser type, general location) when you browse the site.

Information you submit is used to respond to your inquiry, scope a project, or process a job application. Analytics data helps us understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Contact form and call-booking submissions are retained for as long as needed to manage the relationship, and deleted on request. Job applications are retained for 12 months.

We use functional and analytics cookies to keep the site running smoothly and to understand aggregate usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
